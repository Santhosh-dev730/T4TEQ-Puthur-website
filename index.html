About Us

Our Story

Making Technology Simple & Accessible

T4TEQ Software Solutions was founded in 2017 in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India, and evolved into a corporate training and development entity after its 2019 incorporation under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India. As an ISO 9001:2015 certified organization and Best Training Institute in Trichy, we are committed to quality, innovation, and excellence, guided by the motto “Let’s Code”.

We bridge the gap between academic knowledge and corporate expectations. Learners gain practical, job-ready skills that meet industry demands. We emphasize technical expertise and professional competence. T4TEQ empowers individuals and organizations to thrive in today’s dynamic digital landscape.
